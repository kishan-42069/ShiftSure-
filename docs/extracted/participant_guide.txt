Theme 1: Resilient Supply Chains
 
When the world fractures, supply chains must think for themselves. 
➢ When global supply chains break, the human cost is massive. Delays disrupt life-saving medicine, raise food 
prices for vulnerable populations, and wipe out incomes for small-scale farmers.
➢ This supply chain has now  shifted from cost-optimized to resilience-first. Driven by maritime re-routings, 
dynamic tariff policies, geopolitical shifts, and climate shocks, structural disruption is now a constant reality. 
Traditional planning tools can no longer keep pace. 
➢ Supply chain resilience is a top priority for SAP’s global customers. Using industry foundations like SAP IBP , 
Transportation Management, and Ariba, along with next-gen SAP Joule AI Agents, businesses are shifting toward 
autonomous, self-healing networks. This Hackfest gives you direct experience in the exact space where 
enterprise tech is evolving fastest.
➢ This year's SAP Hackfest challenges you to harness Agentic AI to build supply chains that don't just react, 
but autonomously sense, adapt, and recover in real time.
➢ Your solution will leverage a team of specialized AI agents:
• Sensing & Scenario Agents 
• Logistics & Inventory Agents 
• Compliance & Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards

Theme 2: Inclusive Workforce
 
AI is rewriting who gets to work. The question is whether it rewrites the rules fairly.
➢ AI is reshaping global employment, turning traditional hiring upside down and creating a $5.5T global skills gap. 
The challenge is no longer "Will AI take jobs?“ It’s "Who will AI leave behind?“
➢  Human Capital Management is at the core of SAP’s strategic focus. Through SAP SuccessFactors, the Talent 
Intelligence Hub, and next-gen Joule AI Agents, SAP is leading the shift toward skills-first hiring, automated 
talent development, and smart recruiting. This theme isn't abstract. It’s about your future workforce.
➢ Your challenge is to build AI solutions that include, empower, and upskill. Hackfest teams will design a 
multi-agent AI ecosystem where below-mentioned specialized agents collaborate to create a fair, skill-first 
workforce platform:
• Skills Discovery Agent 
• Market Intelligence Agent
• Learning Pathway Agent
• Inclusive Matching Agent
• Employer Readiness & Bias Audit Agents 
• Human-in-the-Loop

From Idea to Prototype: The Two-Round Process
Theme 1: Resilient Supply Chains
Round 1 (Regional College Round): Design Thinking & 
Business Model (No Code)
The Challenge: Select a supply chain scenario (e.g., 
pharma cold chain, disaster relief logistics, or tariff-
exposed networks).
The Deliverable: Perform empathy mapping, identify 
system failure modes, map agent-human interactions, and 
build a Business Model Canvas using the SAP Learning Hub, 
student edition
Round 2 (Grand Finale): Prototyping & Live Demo
Single Agent Prototype: Build a functional core agent 
(e.g., a Disruption Sensing or Scenario Planning Agent) 
using vibe coding and AI frameworks.
Full System Showcase: Demo an end-to-end multi-agent 
network simulating a live disruption (e.g., shipping 
closures or tariff spikes)—showing autonomous rerouting, 
audit logging, human approval, and SAP Learning Hub, 
student edition integration.
Theme 2: Inclusive Workforce
Round 1 (Regional College Round): Design Thinking & 
Business Model (No Code)
The Challenge: Select an inclusion challenge (e.g., 
reskilling displaced workers, matching persons with 
disabilities, or supporting women re-entering the 
workforce).
The Deliverable: Conduct empathy mapping, identify 
systemic hiring barriers, design agent-human interaction 
models, and build a sustainable Business Model Canvas 
using the SAP Learning Hub, student edition.
Round 2 (Grand Finale): Prototyping & Live Demo
Single Agent Prototype: Build a functional core agent 
(e.g., a Skills Discovery or Learning Pathway Agent) using 
vibe coding and AI frameworks.
Full System Showcase: Demo the complete multi-agent 
"Career Orchestrator" end-to-end via a real-world persona 
(e.g., a woman re-entering the workforce in a tier-2 city), 
demonstrating seamless agent collaboration, human-in-
the-loop safeguards, and SAP Learning Hub, student 
edition integration.

Participant Guideline
1. Teams to show all the artifacts and working prototype based on one of the chosen themes
2. Participants should primarily use the links shared with them of the SAP platform & system 
landscape. Can also use other non-SAP tools to build the prototype
3. Each Team will be given 15 mins in Total (10 Presentation, 5 mins Q & A)
4. Use the time allotted to the team judiciously, going overboard on time limits will be 
penalised
5. Keep the demo related artifacts in one laptop, do not waste time switching laptops during 
your presentation
6. Keep the video of the demo ready as backup, you can use the same if your demo doesn’t 
work
7. Keep the laptop charged and if there is a special connector required to connect to 
projector , please bring the same

Additional Notes 
1. Prize Money would be, 75k, 50k and 25k INR (For top 3 teams) in the Grand Finale Round on 4th 
November.
2. SAP would provide learning resources (SAP Learning Hub (Student’s Edition)) to the teams and 
students must register and use them. Any team using the learning from there would definitely 
have an edge during the evaluations. 
3. Teams do not need to follow a certain presentation template, they can be innovative about the 
same. 
4. Just keep in mind that 15 mins of total time will be given - 10 min for teams to present and 5 
min for Q&A.
5)  Teams are expected to be in full attendance (all 4 or 5 team members) are to be present on the 
presentation days.
6) The Core Team (SAP + NHCE), will take all final decisions and calls on evaluation and other 
aspects logistic or otherwise for the teams and jury.
7) Selection of the Top 3 Teams would be decided by SAP based on the Evaluation score sheets from 
Faculties & Industry Partners
